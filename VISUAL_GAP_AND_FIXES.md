Downloads is the closest composition: five rows, selected inspector, source strip and three widgets now follow the reference structure and spacing. Batch now has a real source/result/table/summary composition, but its rows are less dense and its table does not expose every reference column. Other and Settings still have the largest content/composition differences listed above. All six remain **PARTIAL / awaiting owner visual acceptance**, independently of automated results. This label is not completion approval.
